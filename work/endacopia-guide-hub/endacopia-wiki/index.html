Source Checklist

  • Do not copy prose. Use sources to verify facts, then rewrite the answer in the site's own structure.
  • Two-source rule for route steps. Confirm exact route triggers with direct play plus one external source, or two independent external sources.
  • Mark patch-sensitive notes. If a puzzle, achievement, or bug changes after an update, add a fresh updated date.
  • Keep sources visible. Link important guide claims to Steam pages, community walkthroughs, or direct play notes when the route is easy to miss.
