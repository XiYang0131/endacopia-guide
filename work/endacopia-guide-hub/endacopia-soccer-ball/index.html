Quick Answer

For the Endacopia soccer ball timing puzzle, click the ball itself as it reaches the white line labeled kick. The useful target is the moving ball, not the background or the label, and there is no separate keyboard keybind in the current community reports. If the music starts distracting you after 25 points, mute the game audio and follow the animation until you reach 100 points for Footwork Master.

Timing lead, community-reported: one recent player timing post describes an initial click around 1200ms, followed by clicks around 600ms. Use those numbers only as a calibration starting point; they are not independently reproduced on this site.

How To Kick The Soccer Ball

  1. Start the mini-game from Mellow's room. Interact with the soccer ball until the score / high-score interface is visible.
  2. Watch the cue first. Let one cycle pass without clicking so you can see where the ball crosses the kick line.
  3. Click the ball itself. Steam community answers point to clicking when the ball is right below the kick line.
  4. Click once, then reset your rhythm. Panic clicks make it harder to learn whether you were early or late.
  5. Keep going to 100 points. Steam achievements confirm Footwork Master unlocks at a soccer score of 100 points.
