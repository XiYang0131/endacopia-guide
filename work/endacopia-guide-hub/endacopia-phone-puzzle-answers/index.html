Quick Answer

The phone section has six Jobs calls with different choice routes. Start with 865-9961 if you want to protect a long run from the luck-based boss call. The separate 277-5944 number belongs to the Office secret: reveal it with the Telescope, call it from Jobs, then wait under the stairs.

Before You Choose

These choice and point routes are transcribed from a public community walkthrough and are marked replay pending. Exact wording can vary by build or route state. The numbers are in-game puzzle clues; do not call them outside Endacopia.

Exact Choice Sequences

These are in-game dialogue choices

  4. Say the situation is bad.

The other reported final choices are “You do what you can” and “You do what you must.”

How To Reach The Phone

  1. Use the Office computer. Open the Jobs app rather than treating the numbers as a normal menu code.
  2. Start with 865-9961. The boss call is partly luck-based, so it is the safest first test before building a complete point run.
  3. Keep notes per call. Each conversation has its own route; do not assume a choice from one number carries to another.
  4. Back up before a long route. Use the save backup guide if the phone section is part of an Ending C attempt.

Office Secret Number: 277-5944

277-5944 is not one of the six point calls above. It is the Office secret trigger used by the Stay / Ending C route.

Broken Phone Purpose

Do not confuse the Broken Phone with the six Jobs calls or the 277-5944 Office secret. Community reports identify the Broken Phone as a one-use trigger for the hidden Fairy Boss: use the Telescope at night on the Timesville beach to complete the constellation clue, then use the Metal Detector to locate the phone. After the encounter starts, the phone is no longer a normal call item.

Evidence status: the Fairy Boss identification and trigger chain are community-reported and replay pending on our current build. If it does not spawn, record the time of day, Telescope clue state, Metal Detector location, platform, and game version before treating it as a bug. See the player discussion comparing the Broken Phone and Camera.
