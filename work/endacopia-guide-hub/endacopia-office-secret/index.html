Quick Answer

The Endacopia phone puzzle is the Office secret route: bring the Telescope to the entrance area near the sleeping figure, reveal 277-5944, call it from the Jobs app, and decode the Morse-style message Behind Stairs Wait A Minute. Then stand under the stairs near the vending machine for about one minute and finish the hidden symbol sequence.

Spoiler Scope

This page covers the Office secret required for Stay / Ending C. It assumes you already have a near-complete Chapter I save and are deliberately chasing the hidden route.

Office Route Steps

  1. Bring the Telescope. Do not enter the Office route without the Telescope from Timesville; this secret depends on it.
  2. Use it at the entrance area. Place or use the Telescope near the sleeping figure by the train/Office entrance to reveal the hidden phone number.
  3. Call 277-5944. Sit at the Office computer, open the Jobs app, and call the number from there.
  4. Decode the message. xixi's current guide transcribes the Morse-style instruction as Behind Stairs Wait A Minute. Treat the transcription as community-reported until replayed.
  5. Follow the message. Go to the stairs near the vending machine and stand under that stair area for about one minute without leaving too early.
  6. Finish the symbol room. After the hidden route opens, complete the symbol/decryption sequence before treating the Office flag as done.
