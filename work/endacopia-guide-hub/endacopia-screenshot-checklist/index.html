Endacopia Screenshots: Puzzle Clues and Save-State Checklist

Updated September 19, 2026 Troubleshooting Screenshots

Quick Answer

If you are stuck, capture the displayed clue, your inventory, the in-game clock and what happened after your last action. Keep a separate save before changing branches. A picture of the item alone may not show whether the whole secret is finished.

This page helps you record and compare a blocker. It is not a gallery of newly tested solutions; the pictures below are official promotional media, and the linked community guides contain their authors' own examples.

Record the Save State, Not Just the Screen

  1. Record the build and platform. Distinguish the demo from the Steam full game, and note keyboard/controller use if an input is failing.
  2. Write down the last successful action. Include what you clicked or typed and the response, rather than only saying the route is broken.
  3. Keep the original save. Back up before testing another branch; do not overwrite the only failed-state save while diagnosing it.
  4. Protect personal information. Hide account names, Steam overlays and personal file paths before sharing. Keep the clue and relevant interface visible.
