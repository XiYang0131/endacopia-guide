Quick Answer

The Surgeon Q&A is the late Ending C conversation. Finish the three area secrets, follow the Ending C route into Mellow's room, defeat Overclockey, enter the opening in the wall, and interact with the book before speaking to the Surgeon. How To Reach The Surgeon

  1. Complete the three Ending C secrets. Finish the Misery Town, Timesville, and Office secret routes before the final branch. Use the all secrets checklist to audit them.
  2. Continue through the late route. Follow the Ending C path rather than entering the final door that commits another ending.
  3. Defeat Overclockey in Mellow's room. The current public route references describe the harder late fight as the entrance to the next scene; keep a backup save before the encounter.
  4. Enter the wall opening and interact with the book. The Surgeon scene is behind this transition. If the room does not appear, return to the Ending C troubleshooting checklist.
  5. Ask questions before leaving. Treat the scene as a lore and parser segment, not as another combat or Red Ball prerequisite.

Parser Tips

  • Try the complete question first. A public reference reports that a bare name can fail where a phrase such as Who is Rodger? works.
  • Do not assume punctuation is the problem. Public guides report that capitalization is usually flexible, while the wording itself can matter.
  • Try one topic at a time. Ask a character, location, boss, or concept separately so you can tell which prompt produced the response.
  • Keep a save before the conversation. The full prompt inventory and one-time song details are community-reported; preserve a restore point before testing.
