Endacopia Achievements Guide

Updated July 30, 2026 16 achievements Spoilers below

Quick Answer

Endacopia has 16 Steam achievements. The three ending achievements are Escape, Return, and Stay. The most important route achievements for guide planning are System Override, A Monumental Finale, Fatal Performance, Case Closed, Escape, Return, and Stay.

Recommended Unlock Order

  1. Blind story run. Unlock early progress achievements naturally and take notes on optional interactions.
  2. Before the Chameleon fight, die once. This should cover Fatal Performance before you win the fight for A Monumental Finale.
  3. Save before the Saw Box. Use code 471, then branch Toy Saw for Escape and real Saw for Return.
  4. Restore a Chapter I save for Stay. Complete the Misery Town, Timesville, and Office secrets, then finish the late route to Ending C.
  5. Clean up optionals. Check fishing, Roger's bird, easter eggs, Adaminia, and Back Where You Started after the ending achievements are secured.

FAQ

Are hidden achievements shown here?

Yes. This page is for completionists and includes spoiler-sensitive achievement names.

Which achievement should I not miss?

Fatal Performance is easy to miss if you defeat the Chameleon first and never replay the fight. Do the death route once before winning.

Does Stay require the fishing secret?

Yes. Current ending-route sources include the Timesville fishing secret as one of the three required Chapter I secrets for Ending C.
