Quick Answer

For the Endacopia achievement guide, start with all 16 Steam achievements, then plan the four ending unlocks and the boss battle-versus-avoidance pairs. The key pairs are System Override / Short Circuit, Fatal Performance / Cut The Act, and No Strings Attached / Case Closed.

Short Circuit Not Unlocking After Ending C?

Do not treat every skipped AI fight as Short Circuit. Current community reports distinguish two outcomes: the Office phone and symbol-puzzle secret can deactivate the AI as part of the Ending C route, while the achievement may require the separate avoid-fight route. This is a community-reported distinction, not a confirmed universal rule on this site.

  1. Keep the branch save. Save before the Core resolution and copy the save folder while the game is closed.
  2. Separate the goals. Use the Office secret for the Ending C route, but keep another pre-secret save if you are specifically testing Short Circuit.
  3. Do not overwrite a failed state. Record whether the AI was absent, whether the route ended by an intentional skip, and whether Steam showed the achievement.

Evidence state: Steam achievement pairing plus multiple player reports; exact current-build unlock condition remains replay-pending.

New Route Notes From The Community Guide

  • Footwork Master: mute distracting audio if needed and click only when the ball crosses the white KICK line. The target is 100 points.
  • Achievement Unlocked: after getting the Remote Solutions Advisor job, enter the Office and click the wall poster marked ACHIEVEMENT.
  • Against All Odds: the dice route cycles through Suit Guy, Baby, and Printer. Beat Printer to complete the sequence; the gamblers reset afterward.
  • I Want To Be A Fisherman: find Catthew at the cabin, prison, and cemetery hiding spots, escape the cemetery monster, then receive the license and unlock fishing from the diver.
  • Water Break: Office drinking-machine interaction is the stable first checkpoint. The waterfall and third-location wording still conflict across community guides, so record each separate drink and keep the route labeled as needs verification.

Recommended Unlock Order

  1. Play one normal route first. Record optional poster, soccer, water, dice, fishing, and combat progress as you go.
  2. Keep boss-branch saves. Save before AI, Trapezist, and Chameleon decisions so you can unlock both battle and avoidance achievements.
  3. Save before the Saw Box. Use code 471, then branch Toy Saw for Escape and real Saw for Return.
  4. Restore a Chapter I save for Stay. Complete the Misery Town, Timesville, and Office secrets, then finish the late route to Ending C.
  5. Clean up hidden and optional achievements. Finish all fish, dice, soccer score, water interactions, 15 enemy defeats, and the Andy / The Yeti route.

FAQ

Are hidden achievements shown here?

Yes. This page is for completionists and includes spoiler-sensitive achievement names.

Which achievement should I not miss?

The boss avoidance and battle achievements are easy to split across different saves. Save before the AI, Trapezist, and Chameleon resolutions so you can test both outcomes.

Does Stay require the fishing secret?

Yes. Current ending-route sources include the Timesville fishing secret as one of the three required Chapter I secrets for Ending C.
