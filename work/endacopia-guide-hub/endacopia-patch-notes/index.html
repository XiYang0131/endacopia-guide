Quick Answer

Endacopia launched on Steam on July 27, 2026. A public community patch log reports builds through 1.06, but this page does not treat every listed fix as independently verified. Update through Steam before testing a long ending or achievement route, then keep a manual save backup.

Player Checklist Before A Long Route

  1. Let Steam finish updating. Check the download queue before beginning a new Ending C or 100% run.
  2. Keep three backups. Save before the Saw Box, keep a near-complete Chapter I file, and preserve a branch save before boss choices.
  3. Record the build and symptom. A useful bug report includes the version, room, action, save state, and whether a reload fixes it.
  4. Re-test old community fixes. If a guide says a softlock is fixed, verify it on the current build before changing our page from “reported” to “confirmed.”

Why This Page Separates Evidence

Patch pages are useful for players who are stuck, but secondary patch summaries can silently turn into false certainty. This site keeps official Steam links, community reports, and our own replay results in separate labels so a route guide can be corrected without rewriting the whole site.
