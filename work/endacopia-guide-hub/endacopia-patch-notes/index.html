Quick Answer

The official Steam news feed checked September 20, 2026 returned 1.08, posted August 7, as its newest announcement. The older notes below contain useful fixes for missing flowers, toy placement and achievement tracking. These are historical fixes newly checked against their original sources, not new September patches or proof that every current save is repaired.

AI Route Save Warning

Community reports describe an AI skip route that can look like a crash or progress reset when the Office secret is involved. Treat that report as route-specific, not as proof that the save is safe: close the game before copying saves, keep a pre-Office-secret backup, and preserve the post-incident folder if progress appears to roll back.

  1. Do not overwrite the only save. Copy the save folder before testing the outlet, phone, or Office symbol route.
  2. Record the state. Note the game build, whether the AI was absent, whether the game closed intentionally, and what the next launch loaded.
  3. Separate route and bug. Compare the same save against the Ending C and achievement pages before calling it a universal crash.

What These Notes Do Not Resolve

No checked patch note establishes universal Clocky or Henry block timings, an Ending C A/B prerequisite, every Core Key recovery case, or a way to reconstruct an overwritten save. The Clocky opening-fight correction rests on player reports, not a patch: do not restart solely because you fought Clocky. Keep route conditions separate from the specific bugs listed above.

Player Checklist Before A Long Route

  1. Let Steam finish updating. Check the download queue before beginning a new Ending C or 100% run.
  2. Keep separate backups. Preserve the pre-Saw Box state and relevant earlier route branches before replacing a save.
  3. Record the build and symptom. A useful bug report includes the version, room, action, save state, and whether a reload changes it.
  4. Match the exact symptom. A fix for a missing flower is not a promise to restore a key after leaving its chapter.

Why This Page Separates Evidence

Patch pages are useful for players who are stuck, but secondary patch summaries can silently turn into false certainty. This site keeps official Steam links, community reports, and our own replay results in separate labels so a route guide can be corrected without rewriting the whole site.
