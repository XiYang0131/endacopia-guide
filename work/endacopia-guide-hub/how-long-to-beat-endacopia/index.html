Quick Answer

Allow several sessions for a first playthrough. Adventure Game Hotspot's reviewer reports roughly 10 hours; a separate player reports 9 hours blind and 23 hours for 100%. These are individual experiences, not a measured average or guaranteed range. A guided run and an all-achievement run should not share one estimate.

How long is Endacopia?

The review describes an exploratory playthrough of roughly ten hours; its category label says “more than 10 hours.” Those are descriptions of the same review, not two independent measurements. The player's nine-hour blind run offers a second reference, while their 23-hour achievement completion shows how much optional cleanup can add.

Read any aggregate estimate alongside its submission count, route category, and game version. We have not verified a current HowLongToBeat sample or recorded a current-build timed run, so neither is used to establish an average here.

What Adds The Most Time?

  1. Going blind. Endacopia's strongest puzzles are fairer when you track room state, but they still reward observation.
  2. Mini-games. Soccer ball 100 points, dice, fishing, and boss fights can each add retries.
  3. Ending C / Stay. The route requires multiple secrets and can become expensive if you lack a branch save.
  4. All fish. Steam confirms the I Want To Be A Fisherman achievement for catching every fish.
