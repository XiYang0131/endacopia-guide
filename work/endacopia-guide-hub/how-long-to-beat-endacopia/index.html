Quick Answer

Two published first-playthrough references are 9 hours blind and roughly 10 hours exploring. The 9-hour player also reports 23 hours for 100%. Use these as planning examples, not an average or a promised 9–10-hour range: puzzle-solving pace, retries and optional cleanup differ.

Main story or 100%? Choose your goal in the time comparison below. The 23-hour account is total completion time, not 23 extra hours after the first ending. These reports concern the full game, not the demo.

Can You Finish Endacopia in One Evening?

Do not budget a short evening around a rehearsed walkthrough video. For a blind run, the two cited experiences are better planning references. Split exploration across sessions if you want to solve puzzles yourself; use the specific puzzle answer only when stuck. If your goal is one ending, distinguish that from the optional work in the 100% checklist.

Why do other estimates look shorter? Check whether they describe a guided run, a demo, a speedrun, or a small poll. We have not verified a current HowLongToBeat sample, so figures quoted by other guide sites are not presented here as a measured average.

What Adds The Most Time?

  1. Going blind. Endacopia's strongest puzzles are fairer when you track room state, but they still reward observation.
  2. Mini-games. Soccer ball 100 points, dice, fishing, and boss fights can each add retries.
  3. Ending C / Stay. Check the secret-route checklist before counting an ordinary ending as completion of every route.
  4. All fish. Steam confirms the I Want To Be A Fisherman achievement for catching every fish.
