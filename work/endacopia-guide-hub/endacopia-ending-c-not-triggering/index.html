Endacopia Ending C Not Triggering

Updated July 31, 2026 Ending C Stay achievement

Quick Answer

If Endacopia Ending C is not triggering, audit five things first: Escape must be unlocked, Return must be unlocked, the Misery Town secret must be complete, the Timesville fishing secret must be complete, and the Office phone / symbol secret must be complete. At the final door, do not open it. Return to Mellow's room and look for the familiar face that confirms the Stay route is active.

Save Warning

Most failed Ending C attempts become expensive because the player has only one late-game save. Before changing files, close Endacopia and copy the save folder. Then restore from a near-complete Chapter I backup whenever possible.

Three Secret Audit

Misery Town

  1. Metal Detector ready? You need it before finishing the area too far.
  2. Cinema sand checked? The secret starts by scanning the sandy cinema floor.
  3. Remote used on screen? The remote reveals the map you need.
  4. Checkerboard path followed? Photograph or write down the map before leaving the screen.
  5. Figure sequence resolved? The secret is not complete until the hidden encounter sends you back.

Timesville

  1. Fishing paper found? Search the sand with the Metal Detector.
  2. All fish caught? Current route notes describe 18 unique fish.
  3. Reward key received? Do not move on until the fishing completion reward is confirmed.
  4. Monochrome shack revisited? Return to the small shack from the shared-room puzzle.
  5. Windowed-mode step done? Press Alt+Enter when needed, then finish the angel sequence.

The Office

  1. Telescope picked up? Bring it to the Office entrance area.
  2. Phone number revealed? Use it near the sleeping figure to reveal 277-5944.
  3. Jobs app call made? Call the number from the office computer.
  4. One-minute stair wait done? Stand under the stairs near the vending machine for about a minute.
  5. Symbol chain solved? Finish the puzzle sequence after the hidden passage starts.

FAQ

Can I get Ending C on a first playthrough?

Current route sources describe Ending C as requiring Escape and Return first, so do not plan it as a clean first-playthrough ending.

Does the Toy Saw choice matter for Ending C?

After Escape, Return, and the three Chapter I secrets are complete, current route notes say the saw choice is no longer the key Ending C trigger.

What is the most common missing step?

The Timesville fishing secret and Office stair wait are easy to under-confirm. Capture screenshots of those steps if you are building a reliable save route.
