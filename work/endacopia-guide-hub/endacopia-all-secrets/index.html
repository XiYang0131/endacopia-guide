Quick Answer

The high-value Endacopia all secrets checklist is currently three area secrets: Misery Town, Timesville, and The Office. Complete all three before retesting Ending C / Stay, and keep screenshots of the remote map, Fish Paper / Lost Key route, and 277-5944 Office chain.

Do These Count As Achievements?

Some secrets overlap with achievement routing, but the all-secrets checklist is not the same as a 100% achievement checklist. Use this page when your search intent is Ending C, Stay, or secret route. Use the achievement checklist when you are cleaning up Steam unlocks.

FAQ

What are the three Endacopia secrets?

The three main Ending C secrets are Misery Town, Timesville, and The Office.

Do I need every achievement for all secrets?

No. The all-secrets route is about area-secret completion for Ending C; achievements overlap but are not the same checklist.

Which secret should I do first?

Do the secrets in any route-safe order from a backed-up save, but document Misery Town, Timesville, and Office separately so you can troubleshoot the final trigger.
