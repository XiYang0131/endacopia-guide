Endacopia Stay Achievement

Ending CSecret routeUpdated July 31, 2026

Quick Answer

Stay is the achievement for Endacopia's secret Ending C. To unlock it, complete Ending A and Ending B first, then complete the three Chapter I secrets in Misery Town, Timesville, and the Office before returning to Mellow's room late in the game.

Stay Unlock Checklist

  1. Unlock Escape. Finish Ending A using the Toy Saw route.
  2. Unlock Return. Finish Ending B using the working Saw route.
  3. Misery Town secret. Use the Metal Detector in the cinema, get the remote, copy the map, and complete the checkerboard route.
  4. Timesville secret. Catch all 18 unique fish, open the monochrome shack, and complete the hidden sequence.
  5. Office secret. Use the Telescope, call the hidden phone number, wait under the vending-machine stairs, and solve the symbol puzzle.
  6. Late-game return. Ignore the final door and return to Mellow's room to activate the route.

FAQ

Do I need a new game for Stay?

You need access to Chapter I secret routes. A backed-up late Chapter I save is the cleanest path.

Does Saw Box 471 matter for Stay?

It matters for getting Escape and Return first. The secret route itself depends on the three Chapter I secrets.
