What Changed After Release?

The important post-release guide work is not the release date itself. It is confirming full-game-only content: Saw Box 471, Chameleon achievements, Chapter I secrets, fishing requirements, Office symbols, and the Ending C route.

What to Do After Launch

  1. Use the Steam full game. Ending and achievement pages assume release-version saves and Steam achievements.
  2. Check the walkthrough before branching. The Saw Box 471 branch and Chapter I backup saves are easier to plan before you finish the first route.
  3. Keep patch notes in mind. If the game updates after launch, recheck puzzle steps and achievement unlock conditions before following older videos.
