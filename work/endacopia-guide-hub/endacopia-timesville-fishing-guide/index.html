Quick Answer

Lost Key and VHS are separate routes. For the Fish Key / Lost Key search, unlock fishing, catch 18 unique fish across six time slots, then inspect the completed Fish Paper and follow the hidden two-room route. In Act 3, finish the Paisley/Dollhouse reference-room chain for the VHS Tape, then follow the Black Rose chain for the VHS Projector. The Lost Key does not create the VHS.

Steam 1.08 build note: The official Steam update notes say a bug was fixed where the Metal Detector could stop beeping even when an item was still buried. If your detector appears silent, confirm the game is updated before assuming either Timesville route is blocked.

How To Unlock Fishing

  1. Find Catthew. Play hide-and-seek with the red cat at the mysterious cabin, to the left of the village prison, and behind a cemetery tombstone.
  2. Survive the cemetery encounter. The cemetery location leads to a monster instead of Catthew. Run toward the upper part of the village to meet the real Catthew.
  3. Take the Fisherman License. Finish the conversation with Catthew, then speak to the diver on the beach deck. This is the step that unlocks the fishing minigame.

Community route note: xixi's updated Steam guide lists these three Catthew hiding spots and the diver handoff. This section is source-backed but still marked replay pending.

VHS Route: Tape to Projector

Do not use the Lost Key as a VHS prerequisite. The Act 3 VHS route depends on story flags, room states and the Black Rose trigger chain.

  1. Enter Act 3. Give the selected Saw to Roger and begin the late Timesville investigation.
  2. Finish the reference-room chain. In Aquarium, speak with Whiskers and obtain the Paisley party password Yellow. During the day, recreate the Dollhouse reference room completely: table position, microwave state, prepared food or bowl, thermometer, bird image, light and ceiling-fan sticker.
  3. Trigger the VHS Tape. At the first night period, enter the Lodge upper-right room, give Paisley Yellow, then go to the TV House left of the village entrance. The VHS Tape should appear after the required conversation and room state are complete.
  4. Follow the nighttime Cabin chain. At the third night period, use the Telescope on the inaccessible Cabin, solve the four-fossil and rope interaction, then collect the Metal Detector.
  5. Collect the route tools. Use the Metal Detector at the beach to find the Lockpick and Camera. Use the Lockpick on Nibbles' room, investigate the hidden area with the Camera, and complete the Nibbles conversation.
  6. Get the Black Rose. Use the Metal Detector to find Beartha's PGD, return it to Beartha, and place the resulting Black Rose on the leftmost grave in the cemetery. Sources disagree on the grave's character name, so use the position rather than the label.
  7. Take the VHS Projector. Enter the underground route opened by the grave interaction and follow it to the right to collect the VHS Projector.
  8. Play the VHS. Return to the TV House at night, place the VHS Projector on the television, connect it, and insert the VHS Tape. Record the five-position code before leaving.

VHS evidence state: The Act 3 split and room-state checks are supported by the current walkthrough and boss-route references; the Black Rose/Projector chain is supported by community route material and remains replay-pending on this site.

Got The “For U” Tape But Stuck?

The player report behind this query describes the post-AI handoff: the tape is a route clue, not a standalone item to use at the beach. Go back through the Timesville TV House route and make sure the VHS Projector is installed before inserting the tape. If you only have the tape, recheck the Black Rose and Projector steps rather than restarting the save.

Evidence state: Community report plus the existing VHS Projector help route; wording and item placement remain replay-pending.

Lost Key Route: Separate Hidden Room

This is the fishing-based hidden room route. It does not produce the VHS Tape or VHS Projector.

  1. Get the Fishing Paper. Use the Metal Detector on the sand after the Timesville route gives you access to the beach search.
  2. Catch all 18 unique fish. The key route needs the complete six-period collection, not only the normal aquarium progression.
  3. Inspect the paper again. The Fishing Paper awards the Lost Key only after the full collection is complete.
  4. Complete the two-room puzzle. Return to the pair of nearly identical rooms and finish their shared-room interaction before attempting the hidden lock.
  5. Use the smaller room. Enter the smaller room and wait for the reversed voice clue.
  6. Switch to windowed mode. Leave, press Alt+Enter, return to the same room, and use the Lost Key on the revealed lock.

Lost Key evidence state: The 18-fish, two-room and windowed-mode sequence is supported by the current Lost Key walkthrough. Keep a backup save because the site has not yet replayed this route on the current build.

Have Lost Key but no VHS?

That is expected. Lost Key is not a VHS prerequisite and will not create the VHS Tape or VHS Projector. Use the VHS Route above to diagnose an Act 3 VHS blocker. Use the Lost Key Route only when your goal is the separate hidden room.

Route correction: Do not restart a save just because the Lost Key did not make a VHS appear. First check Act 3, the Paisley/Dollhouse reference-room chain, the TV House trigger, and the Black Rose/Projector chain.
