FAQ

Is Scribbly the name requested after drawing the face?

Yes. Walkthrough and player references agree on SCRIBBLY. The flashlight reveals the package clue in the mannequin's room.

Are all character relationships confirmed?

No. The official premise establishes Mellow and the player's role. Late-game relationships and parser responses need their own dialogue evidence; untested details remain labeled.
