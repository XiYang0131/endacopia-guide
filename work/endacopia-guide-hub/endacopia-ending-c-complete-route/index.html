Quick Answer

To reach Endacopia Ending C / Stay, use a backed-up near-complete save, complete the three area secrets in Misery Town, Timesville, and The Office, then return to the final route only after those checks are complete. Public guides agree that the three area secrets are the core requirement; some route notes also warn about Clocky / boss branch flags, so keep a backup before testing.

Page scope: This page is the complete Ending C route and condition checklist. It is not a substitute for the failure diagnosis page or the individual puzzle answer hub. The final trigger and branch combinations remain marked as replay targets until captured.

Endacopia Ending C Not Triggering?

Do not restart the whole game first. Check the three Ending C secrets one by one, confirm the no-Clocky community route if your save is uncertain, finish the Trapezist, Chameleon, and AI branches, then return to Mellow's room instead of entering the late door.

  1. Secret count: Misery Town, Timesville, and The Office must all be complete.
  2. Route state: keep a backup and test the no-Clocky signal separately.
  3. Final return: use the trigger troubleshooting checklist before changing save files.

Current Community Route Signal

xixi's updated Steam achievement guide explicitly says not to fight Clocky from the start. The same guide then routes Stay through the Timesville fishing secret, Misery Town projector map, and Workplace phone/stair secret before the three main bosses. This is the clearest current community route signal we have; keep a clean save because the site has not personally replayed every branch combination.

  1. Skip the Clocky fight. Unlock the early route, give the password CHESHIRE to the bathroom monster, and continue without triggering the opening battle.
  2. Finish the Timesville secret. Get the Metal Detector, dig up the Fish Paper, catch all 18 fish, complete the dollhouse route in windowed mode, speak to the six past-Timesville characters, and solve the cemetery X-ray puzzle.
  3. Finish the Misery Town secret. Use the Metal Detector in the theater to find the Projector Remote, reveal the Mimic Room map, follow it, and complete the four capsule puzzles.
  4. Finish the Workplace secret. Use the Telescope to reveal 277-5944, call it from the Office desk, decode the Morse hint, then wait behind the left stairs for one minute.
  5. Complete the three main bosses. xixi's route notes say to finish Trapezist, Chameleon, and AI after the three secrets are solved.
  6. Use the Toy Saw, then avoid the late door. After Chapter III, give the Toy Saw to the bathroom monster, return to Mellow's room instead of entering the Do Not Enter door, and prepare for Overclockey.
  7. Finish the true ending sequence. Defeat Overclockey, click the book after entering the wall hole, complete the Surgeon conversation, and check for Stay.

Complete Route Order

  1. Make a real backup first. Ending C is expensive to retest because route flags can be late-game and branch-sensitive. Close Endacopia before copying saves.
  2. Confirm your A / B ending state. Steam community guidance frames Ending C as a post-A/B secret route. If your save is uncertain, restore the backup before experimenting.
  3. Do Misery Town. Bring the Metal Detector to the cinema, find the remote in the sand, use it on the screen, photograph the map, and follow it in the checkerboard room.
  4. Do Timesville. Finish the Fish Paper route, catch all 18 fish, receive the Lost Key, then complete the windowed-mode shack interaction.
  5. Do The Office. Use the Telescope to reveal 277-5944, call it from Jobs, wait under the stairs near the vending machine, then complete the symbol sequence.
  6. Return to the final route. If the final-door behavior looks normal, stop and use the troubleshooting page before overwriting your backup.

Branch Flags To Watch

Public sources agree on the three secrets, and xixi's updated guide now explicitly calls out the no-Clocky-fight condition for Stay. Because this site has not personally re-tested every branch combination yet, treat that condition as the leading community route signal and keep a backup before committing.

  • Clocky routeIf you fought Clocky and Ending C fails, restore a backup and test the no-fight route reported by some guides.
  • Boss routeIf a boss avoid/fight branch differs from your save, record it before changing route state.
  • Saw choiceKeep a pre-saw or Chapter I backup so A/B/C testing does not destroy your clean branch.

FAQ

How many secrets are required for Ending C?

Current public route notes point to three area secrets: Misery Town, Timesville, and The Office.

Yes, public guides describe using a backed-up near-complete Chapter I save to complete all three area secrets in the same route window.

Why is Ending C not triggering after I did one secret?

One secret is not enough. Audit all three area secrets, then check branch-save state, Clocky / boss route warnings, and the final-door trigger.
