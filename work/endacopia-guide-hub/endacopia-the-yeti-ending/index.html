Quick Answer

The Yeti is a hidden Endacopia achievement route tied to finding Andy. It is not the Toy Saw Ending A route, not the real Saw Ending B route, and not the three-secret Ending C / Stay route. Treat The Yeti as a separate cleanup target after Escape, Return, and Stay are confirmed on your Steam account.

Evidence Boundary

Steam confirms The Yeti exists as a hidden achievement, and Exophase lists its requirement as "Find Andy." Fandom route notes say meeting Andy starts a dance minigame and unlocks the achievement. We still need our own room-by-room screenshots before claiming a fully verified local route.

What The Yeti Is

The Yeti sits in the same completion conversation as Endacopia's endings because it appears near Escape, Return, and Stay in achievement trackers. The safer way to plan it is as a hidden route achievement: finish the standard ending work first, then hunt for Andyland / Andy as a separate objective.

Unlock Checklist

  1. Confirm the normal ending achievements. Unlock Escape for Ending A and Return for Ending B before treating The Yeti as your next goal.
  2. Finish or at least prepare Stay. Current public notes usually discuss Andy after the main ending cleanup, so keep a save after your Ending C work if possible.
  3. Look for Andyland / Andy. Public wiki notes connect The Yeti to finding Andyland, the creator cameo route.
  4. Complete the encounter if it starts. Fandom notes describe a dance minigame after talking to Andy.
  5. Verify the unlock. Check Steam or your in-game achievement popup for The Yeti before deleting backup saves.

FAQ

Is The Yeti an ending?

It is better described as a hidden route achievement. Players often search it as an ending because it appears beside the ending achievements in completion lists.

Can I unlock The Yeti before Ending C?

Possibly, but this site recommends finishing Escape, Return, and Stay first until we capture a direct trigger route. That keeps completion planning safer.

Does The Yeti require the Saw Box code?

Not directly based on current public achievement descriptions. The Saw Box code 471 matters for Ending A and B routing.
