# Fandom competitor structure review - 2026-08-12

## Scope

Reviewed the public Endacopia Fandom wiki entry and search-result snippets on 2026-08-12. The direct Fandom page was not available to the fetcher at review time, so the observations below are limited to the visible search description and the public URL. The user's traffic observation is not treated as an independently measured traffic figure.

## Raw observations

- The wiki was founded in January 2024, before the current full release, which gives it historical first-mover coverage for early game, demo, release-date, and lore searches.
- The public description positions it as a broad community reference with characters, people, policies, and community content, rather than as one long walkthrough.
- Search snippets still contain older pre-release framing. That is an opportunity for this site to own current full-release intent and clearly separate demo from full-game information.
- Fandom pages can expose many named entities and internal links. That structure can capture searches for a character, location, or item even when the player does not know the final guide URL.

## What to absorb

1. Build a visible entity taxonomy: game facts, characters, locations, items/terms, endings, puzzles, and achievements.
2. Make the taxonomy a navigation layer that routes into focused answer pages.
3. Keep a public correction and source policy so community findings can be added without opening an unmoderated comment wall.
4. Keep historical and current-release scope explicit on titles, first-screen answers, and update notes.

## What not to copy

- Do not copy Fandom prose, screenshots, or page templates without a compatible license and attribution record.
- Do not treat a community wiki entry as official confirmation for version-sensitive routes.
- Do not create a thin URL for every character or object. Add a page only when it has a distinct query and enough evidence to answer it.
