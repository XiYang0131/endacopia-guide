## Review rules

- Group queries by the page that currently receives the impression.
- Flag positions 5-20 with low CTR for title / snippet tests.
- Flag queries with impressions but no dedicated answer for intent review.
- Do not create a new page when an existing page can satisfy the query without
  competing with another page.
