Endacopia Water Break Achievement Guide - Hydrate 3 Times

Quick Answer

Water Break requires three separate drinks. The community-cross-checked route uses the Office water cooler, Timesville waterfall and the bottle in Nibbles' room. The developer's 1.03 notes explicitly name Nibbles' bottle as a hydration interaction and fix it not counting. That confirms this particular interaction; the complete three-stop route remains community-backed, not locally replayed.

Known Requirement

Steam lists Water Break as an achievement for hydrating yourself on three separate occasions. Treat "separate occasions" as three distinct interactions, not three clicks on the same source unless your run proves otherwise.

Community Route Details

  1. Secure the mug first. Take the glass mug from the lower corner of the trash-filled Strategic Probability Analyst area.
  2. Relocate before drinking. Return to the hiring area, request Remote Solutions Advisor, then use the mug on the Office water cooler.
  3. Finish the two Timesville interactions. Drink from the waterfall, then drink directly from the squirrel bottle in Nibbles' room.
  4. Check each separate drink. Do not substitute three clicks on one source. If the bottle does not count, check the version and the other two interactions before assuming the location is wrong.

Nibbles' Bottle Did Not Count

Endacopia 1.03 explicitly fixed Nibbles' water-bottle interaction failing to contribute to the hydration achievement. Check that the update is installed, then revisit the interaction if the route still allows it. The note does not promise retroactive credit for every older save.

If all three interactions still fail to unlock Water Break, preserve the save and record the build and each location. A blanket reset, a different unnamed apartment or a guaranteed old-save repair is not established by this patch note. Last checked September 20, 2026; no gameplay replay claimed.

How To Avoid Wasting A Run

  1. Drink when you first see a source. Do not postpone optional hydration until after area completion.
  2. Screenshot before and after. Capture the interaction prompt and the result or achievement pop.
  3. Keep a simple note. Write down the chapter, room, and whether the source still works after revisiting.
