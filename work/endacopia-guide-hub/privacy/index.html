Privacy Policy - Endacopia Guide Hub

Updated August 7, 2026 Analytics disclosed Unofficial site

Quick Answer

This is a static, unofficial fan guide. It does not require an account or collect guide comments directly. Google Analytics 4 is used to understand page visits and guide interactions so the site can improve.

What Analytics Records

When enabled in your browser, Google Analytics 4 may receive page views, referral information, browser and device information, approximate location, and interaction events such as helpful feedback or guide navigation. The site uses this information in aggregate to understand which answers are useful.

Cookies and Choices

Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, use browser privacy controls, or avoid the site. Blocking analytics does not prevent the guide pages from working.

External Services

Guide pages link to services such as Steam, Reddit, Google, Fandom, and other public sources. Those services have their own privacy policies and may process visits independently. The site does not control their data practices.

Corrections

To request a correction or ask about an image, use the Contact page and the public Steam discussion channel listed there. Do not include passwords, save files, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

Changes

This page will be updated when the site adds a material data-collection method. The current revision date is August 7, 2026.
