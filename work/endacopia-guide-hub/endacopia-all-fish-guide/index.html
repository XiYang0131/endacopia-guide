Quick Answer

To fish in Endacopia, use the Timesville fishing spot after the route opens, then catch 18 required Timesville fish: three catches in each of six clock slots. Catch all 18, leave the fishing interaction, and recheck the Fish Paper to confirm the Fisherman achievement and the secret-route key. If the key is still missing, continue to the hidden two-room/windowed-mode route rather than restarting immediately.

Get The Fisherman License

  1. Find Catthew three times. Check to the right of the unusual cabin, to the left of the village prison, and behind a cemetery tombstone.
  2. Escape the cemetery encounter. The last hiding spot becomes a monster encounter. Run toward the upper village area to find the real Catthew.
  3. Unlock the fishing spot. Catthew gives the Fisherman License; take it to the diver on the beach deck to start fishing.

Fishing Method

  1. Use the correct time slot. Each clock position has its own three catches; do not keep grinding the same slot after it is exhausted.
  2. Cast when ready. Interact with the rod at the beach, cast, and wait for the bite/sparkle cue.
  3. Control the pull indicator. Keep the indicator inside the target zone instead of watching only the fish art.
  4. Exit and confirm. If the achievement does not pop while holding the pole, step away from the rod, exit the fishing interaction, and recheck the Fish Paper and Steam overlay.
