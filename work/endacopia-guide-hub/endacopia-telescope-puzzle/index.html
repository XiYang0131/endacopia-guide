Quick Answer

Take the Telescope to the locked-shed cliff in Timesville. Use it during the night state indicated by the prison clue, then click the large bright white puzzle stars. If the correct location and time still produce no response, refresh the route by leaving the area or restarting from a save.

Why This Puzzle Is Easy To Miss

The Telescope is not a free-form sky viewer. The interaction is gated by both a specific Timesville location and a night-state clue. Small background stars are not a reliable target, so do not brute-force every visible point.

Prerequisites

CheckWhat to confirm
ItemYou have the Telescope and can select it.
LocationYou are at the cliff by the locked shed, not just somewhere in Timesville.
TimingYou have read the prison clue and switched to the corresponding night state.
TargetYou are clicking the large bright white puzzle stars rather than the small background stars.

Step-by-Step Telescope Route

  1. Read the prison clue. Treat its night indication as the timing gate for the cliff interaction.
  2. Switch to the required night. If the game clock is on a different night state, the Telescope may look usable but not advance the route.
  3. Go to the locked-shed cliff. Position the Telescope at the cliff overlook associated with the shed.
  4. Use the Telescope. Wait for the interaction state, then select the large white stars that belong to the puzzle constellation.
  5. Finish the constellation. The route is complete when the star connection or interaction advances instead of only showing the ordinary sky.
  6. Continue to the linked route. The Telescope clue feeds into the Office secret and the 277-5944 phone route; it can also be part of late-game secret or ending checks.

FAQ

Is the Telescope puzzle the same as the Office phone puzzle?

They are linked but not identical. The Telescope reveals the clue used by the Office route; the Office secret page covers the phone and under-stairs sequence.

Does the Telescope work at any time?

Public guides report a night-state requirement based on the prison clue. Do not treat daytime or an arbitrary night as equivalent.
