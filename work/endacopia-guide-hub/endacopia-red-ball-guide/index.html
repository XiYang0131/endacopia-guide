Quick Answer

For the Endacopia operation minigame, hit the Yellow, Pink, Purple, and Red dead clowns in the theater to get the Scalpel. Use it in the three-stage surgery route, then take the Red Bingo Ball to the blue character by the porta-potty and win five in a row. If the operation fails, steer by the effective knife tip and move slowly; the exact current-build hitbox remains replay pending.

What This Page Covers

This is the focused Red Ball route. It does not repeat the whole Misery Town chapter: use the clown theater guide for the seat and story setup, then return here for the Scalpel, surgery, and Red Ball follow-up.

Operation Minigame: Scalpel and Surgery Steps

  1. Finish the four-color theater sequence. Watch the seat or projector clues, then target only the four dead-clown colors listed above.
  2. Use the Scalpel on the patient. Start the operation minigame after the item is in your inventory.
  3. Guide the tip through the open center. Move slowly and judge the small effective tip, not the full blade or handle shape.
  4. Take the route to its endpoint. Corners are where a fast sweep is most likely to touch the boundary. Lower mouse sensitivity if your controls make small corrections difficult.
  5. Collect the Red Bingo Ball. The operation is the item gate; it is not the final five-in-a-row interaction.

Red Ball Follow-Up

Carry the Red Bingo Ball to the blue character near the porta-potty. Start the bingo-style interaction and complete five in a row. The top row is the simplest target to track, but the important check is the five-in-a-row completion rather than a specific row.

FAQ

Do I need the Red Ball for the main ending?

It is an optional puzzle route and can matter for achievement or side-route cleanup. Follow the full walkthrough if you are unsure whether to pause here during a first run.

Can I hit every clown?

For this route, do not. Use the four-color target list and leave the other four colors alone.
