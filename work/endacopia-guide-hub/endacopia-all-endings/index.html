Quick Answer

To get all Endacopia endings, plan for four full-game ending achievements tracked by current Steam achievement sources. Ending A is tied to Escape and uses the Toy Saw route. Ending B is tied to Return and uses the real Saw route. Ending C is tied to Stay and requires both main endings plus one hidden secret in Misery Town, Timesville, and the Office. The Yeti is a separate hidden Andy route, not part of the Saw Box A/B/C branch.

Endacopia Ending Search Shortcuts

Searching for an Endacopia ending usually means one of three things: choosing the saw, unlocking the secret Ending C route, or fixing a missing trigger. Use the matching route below instead of replaying the whole game.

  • Endacopia ending A or B: open the 471 Saw Box guide and make a backup before choosing Toy Saw or the working Saw.
  • Endacopia ending C: follow the Ending C / Stay checklist for the three secrets and final return.
  • Endacopia endings not triggering: use the Ending C troubleshooting guide to audit route flags before changing saves.

Before You Start

Make a save before choosing a saw from Henry's Saw Box. For Ending C, also keep a near-complete Chapter I save before the Misery Town, Timesville, and Office bosses. Close the game before manually copying save files.

How to Get Ending A

  1. Reach Henry's Saw Box. Use Henry's floppy disk on the computer, open PASSWORDS.TXT, and use Saw Box code 471.
  2. Save before choosing. The box contains a toy saw and a working saw, and you should not assume you can take both.
  3. Take the Toy Saw. Give the Toy Saw to Roger when he asks for the saw.
  4. Continue through Chapter III. Finish the Timesville investigation and the Chameleon route.
  5. Avoid the obvious single door. After Roger's late-game conversation, choose the alternate passage instead of the tempting door.
  6. Follow Tip's route. Accept Tip's boost, keep moving forward, clear the final confrontation, and invite the surviving residents before the gathering.

How to Get Ending B

  1. Reload your Saw Box save. This avoids replaying the earlier Office, Core, Scribbly, Misery Town, and boss routes.
  2. Take the working Saw. Give Roger the real saw he requested.
  3. Continue Chapter III. The Chameleon route does not replace the later ending branch.
  4. Choose "Leave It Behind." When the late route presents the door decision, leave it behind and move forward.
  5. Cross the desert. Continue to the ruined house, speak with Roger, then walk to Melo's family home.

How to Prepare for Ending C

Ending C is the secret route. It is not just a different saw choice. Sources describe it as requiring Ending A, Ending B, and three hidden Chapter I secrets.

  1. Finish Ending A and Ending B first. The hidden achievement is Stay, so verify that Escape and Return have already unlocked.
  2. Restore a late Chapter I save. Ideally, stop before the boss encounter in Misery Town, before the boss encounter in Timesville, and before the Office boss encounter.
  3. Complete the Misery Town secret. Bring the Metal Detector to the cinema, scan the sandy floor for a remote, use it on the screen, copy the map, then follow that route in the checkerboard area until the figure sequence resolves.
  4. Complete the Timesville secret. Use the Metal Detector to find the fishing paper, catch 18 unique fish, receive the key, return to the small monochrome shack, switch to windowed mode with Alt+Enter, and proceed until the angel sequence resolves.
  5. Complete the Office secret. Bring the Telescope to the Office entrance area near the sleeping figure, reveal phone number 277-5944, call it from the Jobs app, decode the message, stand under the vending-machine stairs for one minute, and finish the symbol puzzle sequence.
  6. Finish the areas normally. Defeat or avoid the bosses, finish Chapter II and Chapter III, then ignore the door asking to be opened and return to Mellow's room. A familiar face in the room indicates the Ending C route is active.

The Yeti Hidden Route

The Yeti appears in current achievement databases as a separate hidden achievement with the requirement "Find Andy." Public wiki pages describe it as a hidden full-game route where Andyland appears in a Yeti costume after the main ending work, separate from the normal Ending A, Ending B, and Ending C saw-choice structure.

  • Do not combine it with the saw branch. The Yeti is not the Toy Saw, real Saw, or Stay route.
  • Finish the main ending work first. Current public notes point to The Yeti after completing the other ending routes, so do Escape, Return, and Stay before treating it as cleanup.
  • Look for Andyland / Andy. Meeting Andy starts the hidden encounter and unlocks The Yeti achievement according to achievement and wiki sources.
  • Capture this route later. The current page gives the verified broad requirement; we still need our own screenshot of the Andy encounter before publishing a full step-by-step The Yeti page.

FAQ

Does the Chameleon choice decide Ending A or B?

No. The important Ending A/B branch is the saw route and the late door route, not the Chameleon battle itself.

Do I need every fish for Ending A or B?

No. The 18-fish requirement belongs to the Timesville secret for Ending C, not the ordinary Ending A or B branch.

Can the saw choice affect Ending C?

Current sources say the saw choice does not matter for Ending C after all three Chapter I secrets are complete.

How do I get Ending C in Endacopia?

Complete Ending A and Ending B, finish the Misery Town, Timesville, and Office secrets, then return to Mellow's room instead of opening the late door.

Do I have to complete Ending A and Ending B before Ending C?

Current route sources and achievement requirements treat Escape and Return as prerequisites before the Stay / Ending C cleanup route. Keep a backup save before replaying the secret checks.

Is The Yeti part of Ending C?

No. The Yeti is tracked as a separate hidden achievement route. Finish Escape, Return, and Stay first if you are following the main ending branch, then verify the Andy route separately.
