Endacopia Name Puzzle Guide - Flashlight, Box Clue and Room Boundary

Updated September 19, 2026 Flashlight Lore clue

Quick Answer

Enter SCRIBBLY when the mannequin asks for her name after you draw her face. Use the Flashlight around the edges of her room to read the name on the nearby package. This answers the name prompt; it is not the toilet password CHESHIRE.

Verification Community-cross-checked
Last checked September 19, 2026
Evidence Player report and two walkthrough references

Name Puzzle: Clue to Accepted Answer

  1. Confirm the prompt. Finish assembling the face reference and drawing the mannequin's face until she asks you to identify her.
  2. Read the package. Select the Flashlight in the mannequin's room and look beyond the ordinary visible edges. The box is the name clue, not another website you need to search.
  3. Type SCRIBBLY. Use the name at her dialogue prompt. Do not enter it into the toilet or computer instead.
  4. Ask for the map. After she recognizes the name, continue the map conversation. Receiving the map is the useful completion check before returning to the main route.

FAQ

What name do I enter after drawing the face?

Enter SCRIBBLY at the mannequin's name prompt. The nearby package, visible with the Flashlight, supplies the clue.

Is this the same as the Scribbly map puzzle?

It is the name step within that route. After the answer is accepted, continue the map conversation; this page focuses on the Flashlight and name blocker.
